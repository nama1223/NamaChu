## Overview

A tuner for wind instruments.

Its selling point is its ability to display the pitch over time and change, rather than just at the "moment" of playing. This allows you to see things like whether the beginning of a note is too high, or whether you're playing straight.

It also has a function to record your playing and play it back later, and to measure and save the characteristics of your instrument.

### Tuner
Features a ±50 cent meter and time graph display. You can select the range level (beginner/intermediate/advanced/professional) for each instrument (※level settings are not yet complete).

Concert pitch can be set arbitrarily, such as A=442Hz.

### Scale Recording
Records and graphs the scales you play. Saved recordings can be played back and listened to.

It's designed to play back using electronic sounds rather than live recordings, ignoring timbre and allowing you to check only the pure pitch change.

### Instrument Measurement
A mode for measuring the characteristics of your instrument.

The average pitch of three notes played is recorded, and if you play across the entire range, you can see it on a graph.

It allows you to save and load data and compare it with other data, so you can use it to compare instruments or as a reference for coordinating within a part.

### Settings
Instrument, transposition, note name notation, automatic microphone sensitivity adjustment, etc.

### Integrated Version
This app is also included in the integrated app [NamaSound+](/Store/NamaSoundPlus/NamaSoundPlusStore.html) which includes a metronome (NamaMetro) and harmony (NamaHam).
